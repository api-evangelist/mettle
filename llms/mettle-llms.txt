# Mettle

> Mettle is a digital-only business bank account brand for UK sole traders, freelancers, and small limited companies, wholly owned by NatWest Group. As a NatWest Group brand it participates in UK Open Banking through the "Bank of APIs" developer platform, publishing PSD2/OBIE-conformant APIs: a public unsecured Open Data (Product) API plus the FAPI-secured Read/Write family (Account & Transaction Information, Payment Initiation, and Confirmation of Funds).

## APIs

- [Mettle Open Data Product API](https://www.bankofapis.com/products/natwest-group-open-banking/products-and-locations/documentation/mettle): Public, unsecured OBIE Open Data API describing Mettle's business current accounts and locations. Base: https://api.openbanking.prd-mettle.co.uk/open-banking/v2.3
- [Mettle Account and Transaction API (AIS)](https://www.bankofapis.com/products/accounts/documentation/mettle): OBIE Read/Write AIS. Base: https://api.openbanking.prd-mettle.co.uk/open-banking/v3.1/aisp
- [Mettle Payment Initiation API (PIS)](https://www.bankofapis.com/products/payments/documentation/mettle): OBIE Read/Write PIS. Base: https://api.openbanking.prd-mettle.co.uk/open-banking/v3.1/pisp
- [Mettle Confirmation of Funds API (CBPII)](https://www.bankofapis.com/products/card-based-payments/documentation/mettle): OBIE Read/Write CBPII. Base: https://api.openbanking.prd-mettle.co.uk/open-banking/v3.1/cbpii

## Auth

- Open Data API: unsecured, no credentials.
- Read/Write APIs: FAPI-secured OAuth2/OIDC against the NatWest Group OpenID Provider (issuer https://secure1.natwest.com), mutual-TLS (tls_client_auth) with OBIE/eIDAS certificates, PSD2 strong customer authentication. Scopes: openid, accounts, payments, fundsconfirmations.
